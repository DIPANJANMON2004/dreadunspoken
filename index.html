Questions

Before you send

Will my story be used for sure?

Not every story can be narrated, but every story is read. If yours is chosen and you left an email, I will contact you.

Can I stay anonymous?

Yes. Leave the name empty and your story stays anonymous.

Will you change my story?

I only improve how it is told, such as pacing and clarity. The events, people and ending stay as you wrote them.

How long should it be?

Any length. A few paragraphs is fine. More detail helps, like where, when and what you saw.

Is my email shared?

No. Everything you send goes only to me.

The channel

About Dread Unspoken

Dread Unspoken is a YouTube channel that narrates true horror stories told by real people. Every story is a real experience: unexplained night encounters, strange houses, unsettling strangers and events nobody could ever fully explain. If you have a scary true story of your own, you can send it through this page. Stories are read, lightly refined for clarity, and narrated on the channel with your permission. You can always stay anonymous.
